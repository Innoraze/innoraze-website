Works with the data you already have.

Connect your event and tracking data, GPS exports and video. Innoraze cleans it, joins it and runs the models, so every report starts from the same numbers.

What you connect

Event data
Every pass, shot, tackle and carry from your data provider.
Tracking data
The position of all 22 players and the ball, many times a second.
GPS and physical
Distance, sprints and load from your performance staff.
Video
Match footage, so every number links to the moment it came from.

Metrics your staff can explain

Expected goals
The chance a shot becomes a goal, based on where and how it was taken.
Expected threat
How much a pass or carry raised the chance of scoring soon after.
PPDA
Opponent passes allowed per defensive action. Lower means a harder press.
Field tilt
Your share of the touches in the final third.
