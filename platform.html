Match analysis and opposition scouting

Matchweek

Five reports cover the week from the morning after a match to the next kick-off. Each one is built for the people who use it on that day.

WhenReportUsed byWhat’s in it
MD+1 Match report Head coach, analysts Expected goals, shot maps, pressing, territory and set pieces for both teams, with each key moment linked to video.
MD+2 Player reviews Coaches, players Each player’s actions on and off the ball, compared with their season so far. Coaches can send it to the player’s phone.
MD-4 Opposition report Coaching staff How the next opponent builds up, where they press, their set-piece routines and what has changed under their coach, from their last six matches.
MD-1 Team briefing Players Three things to exploit and three things to stop, each with one pitch graphic.
MD Half-time numbers Bench, dressing room Shot quality, territory and pressing for the first half, on a tablet within two minutes of the whistle.

Services

Our engineers do the setup. Your staff use the results.

Every club gets data setup as part of its plan. You connect the sources once, and every match after that runs through the same four steps automatically.

  1. 1

    Connect

    Link your event, tracking and GPS providers and your video. We handle the formats.

  2. 2

    Clean and join

    Matches, players and timestamps are lined up across sources, so every number agrees.

  3. 3

    Model

    Expected goals, expected threat, pressing and physical models run on every match.

  4. 4

    Deliver

    Reports arrive in the app, as PDFs and on staff phones, on the day each one is needed.
