Privacy

Last updated 2 November 2026

This page explains what personal data Innoraze collects through this website, why, and what you can do about it. We have kept it short because we collect very little.

Who we are

Innoraze is based in Amsterdam, the Netherlands. We are responsible for the personal data described on this page. You can reach us at contact@innoraze.com.

What we collect

When you use our contact form, we receive your name, work email, club or organisation, role and anything you write in the message.

When you email us, we receive your email address and whatever is in your message.

When you visit the site, our hosting provider processes technical data such as your IP address so it can deliver the pages and keep the service secure. We don’t use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and our fonts are served from this site rather than a third party.

Why we use it

We use what you send us to reply, arrange a demo and, if you ask for one, prepare a match report. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before a possible contract, and our legitimate interest in answering enquiries. We don’t add you to a mailing list or send marketing unless you ask us to.

Who else processes it

  • Formspree receives contact form submissions and forwards them to us.
  • GitHub hosts this website.
  • Our email provider stores our correspondence with you.

Some of these providers are based in the United States. Where personal data leaves the European Economic Area, we rely on the safeguards the GDPR requires, such as the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses. We never sell personal data.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for up to 24 months after our last contact, unless you become a client, in which case the client agreement applies.

Club data

Match, player and performance data that clubs share with us to use the platform is covered by the client agreement and a data processing agreement, not by this page.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, ask us to limit or stop using it, or ask for a copy to take elsewhere. Email contact@innoraze.com and we will respond within one month.

If you’re unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, or the authority in your own country.

Changes

If we change this page, we will update the date at the top.
